The Traditional Model

Hard Exterior

A strong firewall protects the perimeter, like a castle wall.

Soft Center

Once inside, users are trusted by default. This is the "Soft Chewy Center".

Static Credentials

Access is granted based on simple passwords, not context.

Lateral Movement

The flaw in the traditional model is Trust. If an attacker breaches the perimeter (e.g., via a phishing email), they are "inside" the trust zone.

They can then move laterally across the network to access servers, databases, and files without further checks.

The Catalyst for Change

Why did the perimeter dissolve?

  • The 'office' is no longer a physical place.
  • Data is accessed from home networks, coffee shops, and mobile devices.
  • VPNs provide a tunnel that grants excessive access.

The Core Concept

NEVER TRUST,
ALWAYS VERIFY

Zero Trust assumes the network is hostile. No user or device is trusted just because of their location.

Business Drivers & Benefits

Breach Prevention

Reduce the attack surface and stop lateral movement.

Data Loss Prevention

Granular control over exactly who sees what data.

Modern Workforce

Securely enable BYOD and remote work without VPN headaches.

Compliance

Better visibility and logging satisfies regulatory requirements.

Zero Trust is a Journey

29%

Of organizations already have a Zero Trust model in place or underway.

Key Implementation Steps:

  • Cultural Change: Shift from "Open Access" to "Verified Access".
  • Not Just Tech: It's a mix of policies, technology, and process.
  • Identity First: Create a single strong identity for users and devices.
